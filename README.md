Seven main stories with drawn illustrations — the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya, the Russian warship hit at Benghazi, the drone strike on flats in Dymer, the Cikande candy factory fire, the Democrats' 258-seat House majority, Liverpool 4-0 Manchester United, and BepiColombo entering orbit around Mercury — plus an In Brief column with nine more verified stories (world chess title match, markets, the Mexico cartel capture, China margin cuts, the Gulf tanker strikes, the Hormuz swim, the West Bank, the Trafalgar tree, Hull 2-0 Brighton). Standard library only, no network, fully deterministic — two runs write identical bytes.
